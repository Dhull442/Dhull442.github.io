About me

Hey, I’m Saksham

I’m a software engineer who gets excited about building things that shouldn’t be possible yet.

These days I’m deep in the AI/ML world, building stuff and trying to figure out what actually matters versus what just sounds cool on paper.

When I’m not staring at code, You’ll catch me on a bike racking up kilometers (3,000 so far, thank you my friend, strava), hiking somewhere in the Himalayas, or gaming in my pajamas.

I’m also fairly obsessed with travel. Like, I need to have a trip booked at all times or I can’t function. It’s my version of therapy, knowing there’s an escape plan gets me through the week. I love bouncing between random cafes in new cities and chatting with people.

Also do some volunteering when I can, teaching kids, planting trees, the works. Turns out touching grass (literally) is a good antidote to spending all day thinking about the unavoidable doom of the world and the world’s race to dystopia. Seriously, I’d love to go back to simpler times like 2012 and buy a lot of bitcoin.

Hit me up if you’re building something wild, have an idea that’s genius or unhinged or both, or just want to argue about which game is objectively the best.
