Keeping Context Small: Systems Lessons for AI Agents

November 21, 2026

How do we keep an AI agent effective as its tasks get longer and its capabilities expand? In this talk, I follow a coding agent and explore what systems software can teach us about managing context, memory, and repeated work.

The slides look at ways to keep context focused while improving capacity, with correctness, latency, and cost as the checks on each change.
